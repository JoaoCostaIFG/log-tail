When a user extracts example.tgz , example.txz , example.tbz , example.tar.zst , or example.tzs , the expected output is the archive contents, not an intermediate .tar file. WinZST is tarball-first, not Zstandard-only. Zstandard-compressed tarballs are the preferred default, but WinZST also supports other tar workflows such as gzip, bzip2, XZ/LZMA2, GNU tar, and POSIX tar, while keeping broad archive support inherited from 7-Zip.
