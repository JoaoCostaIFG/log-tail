NetBSD 9.5 released and EOL for NetBSD-9

HNL 147 points · by jaypatelani · blog.netbsd.org · discuss on HN · discuss on lobste.rs

The NetBSD Project is pleased to announce NetBSD 9.5, the fifth (and final) release from the NetBSD 9 stable branch. It represents a selected subset of fixes deemed important for security or stability reasons since the release of NetBSD 9.4 in April 2024. It is fully compatible with NetBSD 9.0. This also marks the end-of-support for all NetBSD-9.x releases and the netbsd-9 branch.
