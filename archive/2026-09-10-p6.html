DTLS Listeners add TLS-style encryption and authentication to UDP applications without changing the datagram transport model. Clients establish a DTLS 1.2 or DTLS 1.3 session with the Listener's assigned domain and port. Proxylity decrypts authenticated application data and delivers the plaintext payload to your configured Destinations . Responses from your application are encrypted and sent back through the same DTLS session. Choose a DTLS Listener when your application already supports DTLS or needs encrypted UDP transport while preserving datagram boundaries. Common examples include RADIUS, IoT protocols, real-time telemetry, and custom request-response protocols that cannot use a stream-oriented TLS connection.
