She assembled a device with a 12-volt battery, a frequency generator, and a speaker that emits 30 pulses per second. That vibration pushes oxygen away from the flames, and the fire simply goes out in five to eight seconds. She tested it over and over, more than 100 times, until confirming that it also worked on wood, flammable liquids, cooking grease, and electronic equipment.
