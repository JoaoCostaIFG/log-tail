Compilers were free to assume it terminates, and some — Clang in particular — would optimize it away entirely, with spectacular consequences : In Clang, this prints “Hello world!” . The compiler removes the infinite loop, main falls through, and the linker-placed unreachable() function executes. This is not a compiler bug — it’s just UB, still better than nasal demons.
