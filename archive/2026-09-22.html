There’s a sneaky new evolution of the “watermark”, let’s call it — the spymark. A watermark is a visible mark embedded in a physical or digital medium to verify authenticity or assert ownership. Google SynthID is a spymark that embeds secret hidden signals “imperceptible to humans” (Google’s own words) into images, audio, text, and video.
