Setting network.http.response.timeout to 0 (or something very big) in about:config seems to make big downloads both resumable and at least a little more stable. I don't understand why this works, but experimental testing supports it. Note that this assumes the server supports resumable downloads; if it doesn't, no settings in firefox or any other browser are going to help.
