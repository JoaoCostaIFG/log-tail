Humans are terrible at being random. Tap or use arrow keys to pick left or right. A Markov chain learns your pattern The predictor builds a frequency table from your recent picks. Given the context of your last 1-4 choices, it looks up what you did next in similar situations and bets on the most likely side. Instead of tracking left/right patterns, Jeffy maps text into a high-dimensional embedding space and trains logistic regression on top.
