How RC2's π table was made

HN 13 points · by KhalidH · khalidhamadeh.com · discuss

The 256 numbers in the key schedule of Ron Rivest’s RC2 cipher were said to come from π, but the method was never published. A card shuffle that uses π’s digits as dice reproduces both of RC2’s tables exactly. RFC 2268 says the 256-byte table PITABLE in RC2’s key schedule is “based on the digits of PI”, but gives no method, and a second table in the same document is given no origin at all. Both are reproduced exactly by the Fisher–Yates shuffle, using the decimal digits of π as its source of randomness: each draw reads exactly three digits, and draws that would bias the result are discarded. PITABLE starts at the leading 3; the second table starts four digits later.
