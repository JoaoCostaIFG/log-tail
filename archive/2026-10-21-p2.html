These keys could allow attackers to issue fraudulent HTTPS certificates for websites they do not control. An attacker who could intercept a user’s connection could use those certificates to impersonate websites and potentially read or modify traffic. Mozilla engineers responded immediately, blocking the affected certificates in Firefox using OneCRL , Mozilla’s centralized list of revoked certificates. OneCRL delivers this protection without requiring users to update or restart Firefox.
