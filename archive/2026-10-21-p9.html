L 1 points · by bogdan · nikhilism.com · discuss

The Go programming language was instrumental in bringing the Communicating Sequential Processes (CSP) concurrency model into widespread use. In the CSP model, channels are usually unbuffered and act not just as a means of communication, but also for synchronization. That is, sending or receiving blocks until the other side is ready, and then the value is moved from one thread to another atomically. In addition, the select statement guarantees that out of all the channel operations in its clauses, only one is selected and the others remain unprocessed. This allows several messaging patterns to be expressed succinctly. CSP is an old idea (from the 1970s), and while Go sticks relatively closely to that model, other languages have taken it further.
