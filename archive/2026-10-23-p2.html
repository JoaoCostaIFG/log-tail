Each disk carries four labels , two at its start and two at its end, and each label keeps a 128 KiB ring of uberblocks. A new uberblock never overwrites the previous one: the uberblock of txg N goes to slot N mod the number of slots (with multihost=on , the last slot is kept for MMP and the ring turns on one slot fewer).
