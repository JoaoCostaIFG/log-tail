Show HN: A Truly Modular Keyboard

HN 291 points · by NotARoomba · github.com · discuss

Designed in KiCad! There are two boards, the Keybit and the Keyword, and both are 1u (19.05mm x 19.05mm). A single key with a Cherry MX switch and a CH32V003. Every side has pogo pins for 3.3V, GND and I2C so it can connect to the keyword or to other keybits, and the pads on the corners are for programming. The main board that the keybits connect to. It is split into two PCBs that stack on top of each other. The top one has the STM32WB55RGV6, the antenna and the flash, and the bottom one has the USB-C, the charger and the buck-boost converter. The 3 pogo pins on the top are for programming the keybits. Custom designed cases in Onshape , the STEP files are in the cad folder.
