It's often cited/recommended as a way to do tilde expansion (e.g. ~/foo to /home/me/foo ): However, wordexp(3) is also dangerous! That last 0 argument is for the flags parameter. By default, all expansion features are enabled, including command expansion : Disabling this requires passing WRDE_NOCMD as a flag. Unfortunately, this palliative at best in some libc implementations: musl implements wordexp(3) by delegating to the system shell.
