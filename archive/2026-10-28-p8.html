Show HN: Aqueous Archive Format

HN 4 points · by lifthrasiir · github.com · discuss

Aqueous is an archive format and an archiver, aqr , built around lossless recompression. Most files people keep are already compressed, by encoders far weaker than what a modern machine can afford: JPEG's and MP3's Huffman codes, deflate in PNG, ZIP, PDF and gzip, LZ4 and Snappy in browser profiles and databases. Aqueous undoes that compression, codes the contents with stronger models, and rebuilds the original files bit for bit on extraction. The format is specified in spec/index.bs (an Editor's Draft, not yet fixed). Aqueous is dedicated to the public domain, or under MIT-0 where that is not possible ( LICENSE.txt ); see CREDITS.md for the projects its ideas come from.
