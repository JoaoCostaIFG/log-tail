The AOT (ahead-of-time) cache is the JDK's newest approach to improving startup and warmup times of Java applications. The idea is simple: You perform a training run of your application, during which the JVM records what your application does, e.g. which classes it loads and links and which methods it calls. When your application is later started, the JVM can skip a lot of the initialization work as it is already done and immediately has all the classes available in a loaded and linked state. In theory, this is a free startup performance boost without having to change anything in your application.
