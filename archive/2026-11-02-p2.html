Nibnib: Image compression with extreme decompression speed

HN 184 points · by raphlinus · github.com · discuss

An image format tuned for extreme decompression speed. This image format originated as an experiment to see what image quality and performance were possible for DVI output on an RP2350 microcontroller. On devices of that class, it's common to use low bit depth images and palette lookup. There's a budget of approximately 6 cycles per pixel, and that's enough for a pixel fetch, palette lookup, and store into the line buffer, but not a lot of room to spare. The nibnib format uses a palette at its core, but surpasses palette images in quality because the palette stores a delta rather than the absolute color, vastly improving soft regions.
