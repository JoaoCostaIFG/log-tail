Abstract: Ideal hardware performance counters provide exact deterministic results. Real-world performance monitoring unit (PMU) implementations do not always live up to this ideal. Events that should be exact and deterministic (such as retired instructions) show run-to-run variation and overcount on x86_64 machines, even when run in strictly controlled environments. These effects are non-intuitive to casual users and cause difficulties when strict determinism is desirable, such as when implementing deterministic replay or deterministic threading libraries. We investigate eleven different x86_64 CPU implementations and discover the sources of divergence from expected count totals.
