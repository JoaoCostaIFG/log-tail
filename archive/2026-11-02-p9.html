There’s a microchip in my toothbrush. But WHY?

L 2 points · by fanf · youtu.be · discuss

Why is there a microchip in my toothbrush? Hiding in my toothbrush head is a tiny NFC chip, so I built a simple reader with an ESP32-S3 and a PN532 to find out what's on it. It turns out the brush head logs its own run time, brushing intensity, brush type, and even a web address! I ran a few tests to see whether it really tracks pressure (it seems to!). Then I wrote to the chip, reset the head to 50% use, and used my old toothbrush head to unlock my Mac!
