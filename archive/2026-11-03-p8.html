A Neki shard consists of one Postgres primary and multiple replicas. The primary accepts writes; the replicas do not. From time to time, we need one of those replicas to take over as the primary. We may be replacing a (Kubernetes) pod, upgrading the kernel, or moving the primary to another cell. This is what we call a planned switchover . As opposed to an unplanned failover , which happens when there is unexpected server disruption, it is a routine operation that comes at an unavoidable cost: there is some period of time where Neki cannot reliably route writes to either the old or the new primary.
