The LISP curse is often quoted as the reason why so few libraries and reusable components exist for LISP dialects. According to the concept, LISP is so powerful and flexible that it allows developers to create custom solutions easily, leading to them working longer in isolation. This causes them to often reinvent the wheel, because creating code to solve your own problems can be easier / faster than relying on the libraries (and way of thinking) of other people. This leads to a lack of central frameworks and libraries, like many other language ecosystems have.
