All general squircles are parametrized, and can vary between circle and square. The best known squircle is the superellipse, popularized by Piet Hein. The superellipse has the formula $|x|^n + |y|^n = 1$ (we’ll use a unit radius for convenience). When $n=2$ it is a circle, and it approaches a square asymptotically as $n \rightarrow \infty$.
