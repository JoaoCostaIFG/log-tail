Show HN: Robotics needs better CAD, so I wrote a B-rep kernel and 3,487 tests

HN 32 points · by atomarch · springcraft-ai.github.io · discuss

A boundary-representation (B-rep) kernel. Booleans, fillets, sweeps and STEP on exact geometry, accurate to 1e-7 mm, with every result validated. Surfaces are analytic or NURBS, not meshes, so volume, area, and mass properties are computed from the real shape. Every result is checked for validity, and if it fails you get an error instead of a broken solid, so nothing downstream gets built on a bad shape. It has the fastest median case time on brep-conformance, with public results anyone can check. It's pure Rust with no dependencies and no unsafe code, and it compiles to WebAssembly.
