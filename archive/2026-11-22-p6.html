Most of your computations might still be valid, and quite often it is the case that you only need to redo a small chunk of changes. This concept usually works the best when you need to compute a lot of stuff, and there are a lot of inputs, and these inputs change independently with different degree of likelihood. Probably, the most common example is incremental compilation, which makes the compilation reuse as much work as possible, making iterations during development much faster.
