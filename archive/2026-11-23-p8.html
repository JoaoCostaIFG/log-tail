Swapping Jolt's sorted collections to a B+tree

L 4 points · by Yogthos · yogthos.net · discuss

Until recently, sorted-map and sorted-set in Jolt were a straight port of Clojure's PersistentTreeMap , which is backed by a persistent red-black tree. Using a red-black tree has worked well for Clojure, and it provides a solid foundation for backing these data structures. However, red-black trees have a downside, which is that every entry gets its own small node, meaning that every step of a lookup follows a pointer to a different place in memory. So, naturally I got curious to see if a different data structure could be used for the same job which might have a lower overhead. Turns out that a red-black tree can indeed be swapped out for a B+tree here.
