A reader recently wondered about the details of a documented but extremely obscure instruction, FRSTPM. This instruction is documented to exist on the Intel 80287XL math coprocessor, as well as the Intel M80287. The FRSTPM instruction was not present on the original 80287. To understand what FRSTPM does, let’s first look at its older and definitely useful sibling, FSETPM. The original 8087 tracked a 20-bit linear address of floating-point instructions and operands, a side effect of how the 8087 interfaced with the 8086/8088.
