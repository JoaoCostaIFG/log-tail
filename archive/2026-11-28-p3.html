Show HN: Luciole Mono – terminal font designed for legibility

HN 107 points · by BaudouinVH · scriptor-pro.github.io · discuss

Type your own text. 11 pt (15 px) is a particularly crisp size. The zero is slashed and narrower than the O; i , j , l and r are redrawn with serifs to fill their cell. Luciole was designed for visually impaired readers by Laurent Bourcellier and Jonathan Perez, at the Centre Technique Régional pour la Déficience Visuelle ( typographies.fr ). Luciole Mono keeps its design and fits it to a 620-unit grid: wide letters are compressed then thickened back, narrow letters are redrawn, and the bold is a lighter semi-bold so that counters stay open. Luciole Mono is a derivative of Luciole . It is an independent project, neither made nor endorsed by the authors of Luciole. Known limitations and the full list of changes are in the README .
