Cyclomatic Complexity in C#

HN 58 points · by gone35 · blog.ndepend.com · discuss

Improve your .NET code quality with NDepend Cyclomatic Complexity (or CC ) in C# is a code metric that counts the number of linearly independent execution paths through a method. Concretely, it is computed as 1 plus the number of branching constructs in the method body (such as if , while , for , case , && , || , ?: and ?? ). The higher the score, the harder the method is to read, test and safely change. A score of 1 means a single straight path, around 10 is the traditional upper bound recommended by Thomas McCabe, and anything above 25 is flagged as excessive by Microsoft’s CA1502 analyzer.
