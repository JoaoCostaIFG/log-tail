Extract huge archives in place. The archive shrinks as your files hatch, so you only need free space for the output, not for the output and the archive. A 180 GB game download that unpacks to 200 GB needs 380 GB of disk with WinRAR, 7-Zip or unzip , because the archive and everything extracted from it have to exist at the same time. HatchZip needs a little over 200 GB. It checks that the extraction will fit before it changes anything, keeps a safety margin of free space the whole way through, and can be stopped or crash at any moment and pick up where it left off. HN 3 points · by unkn0wn_root · github.com · discuss

cascache is a read-through cache for Go. It caches values loaded from a database or another slow source. Once you invalidate a key, a load that was already running cannot put the old value back. It works in one process, or across processes that share invalidations through Redis. With a plain cache, a read that misses loads the value from the database and writes it to the cache. An update writes the database and deletes the cached key. If a slow read loads the old value before an update and writes it after the delete, the cache serves that stale value until its TTL ends. cascache gives each key a token called a fence.
